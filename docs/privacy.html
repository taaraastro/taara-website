Privacy policy

What we collect

The birth details you enter (name, date, time and place of birth) and your settings are stored in your own browser on your device. We do not need an account to use Spatik Astro.

When data leaves your device

  • AI answers: when you ask a question, your question and the computed chart are sent to our AI provider to write the answer.
  • Voice: if you use voice, your recording is sent to our speech provider to turn it into text, and the answer text is sent to make speech.
  • Payments: payments are handled by our payment provider (for example Razorpay). We never see your card or UPI PIN.
  • Advertising: free users may see ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ads Settings. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com.
  • Analytics: we may count visits with privacy-friendly analytics.

Your choices

Use "Start as a new person" in the Plus tab, or clear your browser data, to delete everything Spatik Astro saved on your device. Contact us to ask about any data held by our providers.

Children

Spatik Astro is not meant for children under 13. Payments are for users aged 18 and over.
