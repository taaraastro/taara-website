About Spatik Astro

Spatik Astro is a free Vedic astrology app for India. It makes your Janam Kundli, daily rashifal, Panchang and 36-guna Kundli matching, and its AI astrologer answers questions about career, marriage, money, health and family from your own chart, with exact Vimshottari dasha dates.

Calculations run on standard astronomical models with the Lahiri ayanamsa. Spatik Astro answers in English, Hinglish and 35 Indian languages, in writing and by voice.

Astrology is offered for guidance and self-reflection. Spatik Astro does not replace medical, legal or financial advice.

Spatik Astro is run by Spatik Astro.
