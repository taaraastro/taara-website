About Taara

Taara is a free Vedic astrology app for India. It makes your Janam Kundli, daily rashifal, Panchang and 36-guna Kundli matching, and its AI astrologer answers questions about career, marriage, money, health and family from your own chart, with exact Vimshottari dasha dates.

Calculations run on standard astronomical models with the Lahiri ayanamsa. Taara answers in English, Hinglish and 35 Indian languages, in writing and by voice.

Astrology is offered for guidance and self-reflection. Taara does not replace medical, legal or financial advice.

Taara is run by Taara Astro.
