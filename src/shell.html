Is Taara really free?

Yes. Kundli, daily horoscope, Panchang, Kundli matching and your first questions to the AI astrologer are free. Extra questions start at ₹49 for 15, with no subscription needed.

How accurate is the Kundli?

Planet positions are calculated on your device with standard astronomical models and converted to the sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsa, the system used in Indian Panchangs. An exact birth time gives the most accurate Lagna and dasha dates.
